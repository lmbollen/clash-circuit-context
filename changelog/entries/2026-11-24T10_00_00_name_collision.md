# FIXED
A signal is never left out of a waveform because its name is already in its
scope. When the duplicates of a name are numbered, and a number gives a name
that the scope already has (two `x` of a helper beside the elements `x_0` and
`x_1` of a pair `x`), the duplicates get two underscores (`x__0`, `x__1`), or
more until no name is taken. Before, the VCD, the FST and the sidecar showed
the pair's elements and not the helper's two `x`, with no warning.
