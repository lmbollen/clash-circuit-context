# ADDED
A step function whose type has no `HasProbe` probes nothing, and that was
silent. Given to `mealyProbed`, `mooreProbed`, `mealyBProbed`,
`mooreBProbed` or `probeFmap`, it is now reported in
`-Wx-circuit-context-uninstrumented`, with the signature that probes its
local bindings (`step :: (HasProbe) => ...`). A step function with
`HasProbe` in its signature is not reported, also when it has nothing to
probe, and neither is one that calls `probe`.
